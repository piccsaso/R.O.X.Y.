# Jezero walk site comparison

## Methodology

Research period: November 2026. Checked NASA Photojournal, the raw gallery, the PDS Imaging Node directory and Atlas API, engineering products, and NASA's rover waypoints. Counted acquisition clocks within site and drive, collapsing left/right meshes, thumbnails, revisions, and alternate projections. A count of archived stereo-derived acquisitions is a screening count. The final usable count comes from measurements that contributed to the exported crop. Matching site frame and sampled height agreement were required before fusing.

Queries included `bundle_id:mars2020_navcam_ops_mesh AND archive.name:*_1709_*` in PDS Atlas, `mars2020_navcam_ops_mesh data mirror usgs ames sol 00477` in web search, and per-sol PDS directory checks. Some older public routes redirect to a generic volumes page. Atlas release URLs provided the Sol 1709 records and representative mesh bytes. The legacy camera-spec PDF returned 403 in the web fetch. USGS catalogs a 1 m HiRISE elevation mosaic and 25 cm orthoimagery for landing-site context. Those scales are suitable for context around the 7 m crop. The original comparison used rover data. The current export includes inferred ground and texture where coverage is sparse. [USGS HiRISE product record](https://www.usgs.gov/data/mars-2020-terrain-relative-navigation-hirise-dtm-mosaic)

## Selected products

[sources.json](sources.json) lists exact product IDs and NASA download URLs. [terrain.json](terrain.json) records checksums, motion counters, PDS references to the source XYZ and texture products, and geometry and atlas contribution counts. The three broad overlap wedges were:

- Sol 474: `NLF_0474_0709026896M746RAS_N0260850NCAM02474_0A0195J02`
- Sol 477: `NLF_0477_0709297838M897RAS_N0261004NCAM02477_0A0195J02`
- Sol 501: `NLF_0501_0711420522M006RAS_N0261110NCAM00301_0A0295J02`

The 25 cm cells were rounded from original site-frame X/Y coordinates, with median Z computed per wedge in each cell. Broad coverage does not establish complete rock faces. The initial crop excluded the sol 501 wedge because it had no valid contribution in that footprint. The expanded build tests this wedge again with additional views from sols 474, 477, and 501. Its actual source contributions are recorded in the current manifest.

## Confidence & Gaps

High confidence in the product provenance, frame, measured overlap, and common-scale mesh. Moderate confidence in visual fidelity at eye level. The local flat slab is a recognizable feature of the Skinner Ridge workspace, with no verified individual NASA feature name or independently verified face-by-face match to the Mastcam-Z mosaic. The original comparison used surface observations only. The current viewer includes inferred terrain and a decorative horizon.
